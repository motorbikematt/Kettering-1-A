What is a Precinct Captain?

Your neighborhood's voice

A Precinct Captain is the Democratic Party's direct link to voters in a specific neighborhood — the boots-on-the-ground contact for our precinct.

Keep neighbors informed

Share factual information about candidates, local ballot issues, and how to register — so every neighbor has what they need at election time.

Get out the vote

Knock doors and make calls before elections — from school board races to presidential contests — to make sure registered voters actually show up.

Build community power

By connecting neighbors and relaying feedback to the county party, Precinct Captains strengthen local civic infrastructure from the ground up.
